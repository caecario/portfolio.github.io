01 — About

Ten years between policy and the people that policy lands on.

I work in Human Resources at Trans Retail Indonesia, where the distance between a written policy and a store team at 6 a.m. is the entire job. Most of my work happens in that gap: translating regulation into something a supervisor can actually run, and translating what happens on the floor back into decisions management can act on.

My approach is process first. Before adding a tool or a training programme, I map how the work actually flows, where it stalls, and who absorbs the cost of the stall. Nine times out of ten the fix is structural, not motivational.

Outside the office I study Islam on the manhaj of the salaf — the Qur'an and the Sunnah as the early generations understood them. It shapes how I handle amanah, fairness in wages, and how I speak to people when the news is bad.
